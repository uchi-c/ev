What we build

A modular block system — configured to your site, your programme, your budget.

Every EBM building starts from one repeatable unit: a 3 m × 6 m steel frame clad in insulated sandwich wall panels, 3 m internal height. Combine units side by side, stack them up to three storeys, or fit them out as a single self-contained space.

Because the units are standardised, we deliver faster than conventional construction — and because they’re modular, the same building can be dismantled, relocated and reconfigured when your needs change.
