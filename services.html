Services

Six services. One accountable contractor.

Take the full turnkey package, or just the part of the job you need. EBM covers the modular building lifecycle from ground works to relocation.

01

Site preparation & civils

A level, drained, load-ready base — before a single unit arrives.

  • Site clearing and leveling
  • Concrete foundations and plinths
  • Drainage and stormwater
  • Slabs and hardstanding
02

Mechanical, electrical & plumbing

A full working fit-out, not just a shell.

  • Electrical reticulation and lighting
  • Air-conditioning
  • Water supply and reticulation
  • Internal drainage and sanitary fittings
03

Turnkey project delivery

One point of responsibility, from brief to handover.

  • Planning and design
  • Procurement and logistics
  • Installation on site
  • Commissioning and handover
04

Modular maintenance

Keeping buildings sound and looking new for years.

  • Structural repairs
  • Panel replacement
  • Refurbishment and re-finishing
  • Preventative maintenance programmes
05

Relocation services

Because the building can move when the project does.

  • Dismantling
  • Transport
  • Reinstallation
  • Reconfiguration to suit the new site
06

Rental & purchase

Own it outright, or rent for as long as you need it.

  • Short-term rental
  • Long-term rental
  • Outright purchase
  • Turnkey delivery on either route

Not sure which services your project needs? We’ll scope it with you.
