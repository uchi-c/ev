About EBM

A modular building contractor built for Zambian projects.

Evaregi Building & Maintenance Co. Ltd (EBM) designs, supplies, installs and relocates prefabricated modular buildings — quick space, delivered affordably.

Who we are

Standardised units. Flexible outcomes.

EBM is a Zambian-citizen-owned contractor specialising in prefabricated modular buildings. We work from a single repeatable unit — a 3 m × 6 m steel frame clad in insulated sandwich panels — and combine, fit out and stack those units into offices, accommodation camps and classrooms.

That approach lets us move quickly: less time on site, predictable costs, and buildings that can be dismantled and relocated when a project moves or a need changes. We handle the whole chain — from ground civils to commissioning and handover, and the maintenance and relocation that follow.

Our mission

“We provide quick space, affordably.”

We exist to give organisations usable, well-built space in a fraction of the time conventional construction takes — without cutting corners on structure, insulation or finish. Whether you rent or buy, the goal is the same: the right space, on site, working, sooner.
