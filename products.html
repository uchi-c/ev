One unit. Endless configurations.

The EBM building system is built around a single standardised module. Learn the spec, then combine, stack and fit it out to suit the job.

The standard unit

3 m × 6 m, steel frame, insulated panels.

A structural steel frame clad in insulated sandwich wall panels, with a 3 m internal height. Strong enough to stack up to three storeys, light enough to relocate, and modular enough to combine into almost any layout.

  • Footprint: 3 m × 6 m standard module
  • Internal height: 3 m
  • Structure: steel frame
  • Walls: insulated sandwich panels
  • Stacking: up to 3 storeys
  • Layouts: combine multiple units into custom configurations
