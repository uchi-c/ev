Sectors

Built for the way each sector works.

The same modular system, configured three very different ways — for commercial teams, remote workforces and learners.

  • 01Office & Commercial
  • 02Accommodation & Camps

01 · Office & Commercial

Offices that go up in weeks, not seasons.

Open-plan working areas, management offices, boardrooms, reception and canteen space — combined and stacked up to three storeys where the footprint is tight. A fast, professional workplace that can grow or move with the business.

For clients who don’t need the building to be relocatable, we also build permanent panelised “site builds” on a concrete raft foundation — the same panel system, fixed in place for good.

  • Open-plan floors
  • Management offices
  • Boardrooms
  • Reception
  • Canteen
  • Up to 3 storeys
  • Permanent raft-foundation option
02 · Accommodation & Camps

Complete camps for remote and on-site workforces.

Self-contained staff housing built to keep a workforce comfortable, safe and productive far from town — with all the supporting facilities a camp needs. Units are self-contained and reconfigurable, so the camp can expand, contract or move as the project does.

  • Staff housing
  • Ablutions
  • Kitchens
  • Laundry
  • Security
  • Recreation
  • Reconfigurable
03 · Education

Classrooms designed for learning, in any climate.

Modular classrooms sized for 30–40 learners, with natural light and convection ventilation to keep the room comfortable, and thermal insulation that holds up in extreme heat and cold. Hard-wearing vinyl floors and aluminium windows finish a room that’s ready to teach in.

  • 30–40 learners
  • Natural light
  • Convection ventilation
  • Thermal insulation
  • Vinyl floors
  • Aluminium windows

Whichever sector you’re in, we’ll match the build to it.
